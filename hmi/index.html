What the colour means

  • live — every point arrived recently and reads good
  • stale — missed a heartbeat, but the site is still reporting
  • comms-lost — the device is not answering, or the site has gone silent
  • not instrumented — no points to trust or distrust

A value that simply has not changed is not stale. The poller applies a deadband — about a third of all readings never leave the site — and re-sends an unchanged value once a minute so that "steady" can be told from "gone". Trust comes from the quality the edge collector attached, not from how new the number is.
